The same pattern on the map. This is a level plot of a LOESS surface fitted to station locations. The northern foothills, Eunpyeong, Jongno, Seongbuk and Nowon, are dark, and so is the south-east edge of Songpa near Wirye, where stations sit at 56 to 71 metres and are empty 45 to 60% of the time. The flat riverside districts such as Gangseo and Yeongdeungpo are pale. So shortages sit on the hills. Now, where are the stations someone would want to cut?
