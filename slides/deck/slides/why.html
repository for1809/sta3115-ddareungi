When a network shrinks, the first idea is often to merge the least-used stations.

But ride counts depend on supply. People who find no bike leave no record.

STA3115 Exploratory Data Analysis, Group 1

Ttareungi rides fell from 44.9 million in 2023 to 37.4 million in 2025, two years of decline. The city is studying the right size of the system again. When you want to cut, you look at the least-used stations first. But a ride only happens if there is a bike. Someone who walks to an empty station and gives up leaves no trace in the data. So before cutting anything, we checked where bikes are actually short.
