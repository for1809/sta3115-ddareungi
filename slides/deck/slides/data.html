STA3115 Exploratory Data Analysis, Group 1

Everything here is public data. Two sources do the main work: the trip records tell us who rode from where to where, and the hourly inventory snapshots tell us how many bikes each station had. On top of that we added hourly rain from the Korea Meteorological Administration, elevation and bare-earth terrain, and living population. The trip and inventory data use different station IDs, so we matched them through the real-time rental API.
