STA3115 Exploratory Data Analysis, Group 1

Check two, slope. You might think hills matter because they are steep. But slope has no direction. Picture two stations on the same steep hill, one at the top and one at the foot. Both sit on steep ground. In the morning people ride down, so the one at the foot fills up and the one at the top empties. Slope cannot tell them apart; height can. That is what the left panel shows: once we account for elevation, what is left does not change with slope, the line is flat. What does add something is the climb, how many metres the trips arriving at a station had to go up. A station that people must ride up to is refilled less, and the right panel rises. Model fit says the same: slope alone is the weakest, elevation is much better, and elevation plus climb is best.
