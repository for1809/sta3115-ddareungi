STA3115 Exploratory Data Analysis, Group 1

Check two, slope. You might think hills matter because they are steep. But slope has no direction. Here are two real stations on the same hill in Geumho-dong, Seongdong, 780 metres apart. Singeumho Station exit 2 is near the top at 68 metres; Geumnam Market crossroads is near the foot at 24 metres. The ground around both is equally steep, about 9.5 degrees. Yet the top one has no bikes in 80% of morning commute hours, and the foot one in 0.5%. Riders reaching the top station have climbed 26 metres on average; riders reaching the foot station came down. And the top one gets 2 rides a day, so by ride counts alone it would look like a station to merge. The figure shows the same thing for all stations: once we account for elevation, what is left does not change with slope, the left line is flat, but it rises with climb. Model fit agrees: slope alone is the weakest, and elevation plus climb is best.
