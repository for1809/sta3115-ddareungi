STA3115 Exploratory Data Analysis, Group 1

Objection one, slope. We first explained shortage with elevation, then plotted what was left against slope and against climb. If slope mattered on its own, the left curve would rise. It is flat. Against climb, meaning how many metres the trips arriving at the station had to go up, the residual does rise. AIC agrees: slope alone is the weakest terrain model, and elevation plus climb is the best. Being high up and having to ride up to get there both go with shortage.
