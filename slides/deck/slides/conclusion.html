CONCLUSION

Before cutting stations, check whether the hills are short of bikes

1. Morning shortage rises with elevation and climb, and is not explained by subway access, rain or housing.

2. Of 427 low-use merge candidates, 128 already run empty in the morning. At 40 m and above, half do.

3. Proposal: when choosing stations to merge, look at morning shortage as well as ride counts, and try moving bikes up the hills before the morning peak.

To sum up. Morning shortage rises with elevation and climb, and subway access, rain and housing do not explain it. Of the 427 low-use stations that look like merge candidates, 128 already run empty in the morning, and at 40 metres and above it is half. So our proposal: when choosing stations to merge, do not look at ride counts alone. Look at morning shortage too, and for hill-top stations, try rebalancing bikes before the morning peak before you cut them.
