STA3115 Exploratory Data Analysis, Group 1

Objection two, rain. When it rains at commute time, morning rentals drop by 51 to 89% depending on how much it rains. If shortage were driven by that day’s demand, hill-top stations should be much less empty on wet mornings. This Tukey mean-difference plot compares each station on wet and dry mornings, and the differences sit near zero. At 40 metres and above, shortage only drops from 25.1% to 20.9%, and the same-station correlation is 0.85. Demand falls, but they still run empty: the bikes simply are not put back up the hill before the morning.
