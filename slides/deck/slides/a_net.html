APPENDIX 2

Hill-top stations lose bikes over the day

Net change = returns − rentals. Below zero, the station loses bikes over the day.

The curve drops below zero from about 30 m: people ride down, and ride back up less.

STA3115 Exploratory Data Analysis, Group 1

This is why hill-tops run empty. Daily returns minus rentals drops below zero from about 30 metres. More bikes leave hill-top stations than come back.
