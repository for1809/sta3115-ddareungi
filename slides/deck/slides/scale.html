STA3115 Exploratory Data Analysis, Group 1

First, scale. For each weekday hour we stacked bikes parked at stations and bikes being ridden. Even at 6 p.m., the busiest hour, only 3,915 bikes are moving, 11% of the fleet; the other 89% are standing at stations. On these numbers alone it looks like there are too many bikes. Whether the parked bikes are where people need them is a different question.
