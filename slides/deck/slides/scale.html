Backup for the conclusion. For each weekday hour we stacked bikes parked at stations and bikes being ridden. Even at 6 p.m., the busiest hour, only 3,915 bikes are moving, 11% of the fleet; the other 89% are standing at stations. So the fleet is large enough; the problem is where the bikes are.
