STA3115 Exploratory Data Analysis, Group 1

This slide builds on our teammate’s two-by-two idea. Looking at stock alone, many stations seem to recover by the evening. But stock also moves when trucks rebalance, so we matched each day’s stock with the trip records. Between 7 and 9 a.m., stock changes match rides almost exactly, a correlation of 0.99, so almost nothing is refilled during the peak. Over the whole day, the picture flips with elevation. Below 20 metres, riders bring in about one bike a day and other moves, mostly trucks, take about one away. At 40 metres and above, riders take away about four bikes a day, and other moves bring almost exactly four back; 89% of hill-top stations are drained by riders. So the hills are already refilled every day, and they are still empty a quarter of commute hours. The question is not whether to refill them, but when.
