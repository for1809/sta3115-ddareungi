STA3115 Exploratory Data Analysis, Group 1

At the district level, flatter districts have more rentals per station: each extra degree of mean slope means 17.5% fewer. The three busiest districts, Gangseo, Songpa and Yeongdeungpo, average 1.6 degrees, less than half of the rest. So slope goes with how much people ride, while elevation and climb go with where bikes run out. With only 25 districts, and some like Gangnam, Seocho and Yongsan riding less than their slope suggests, we treat this as a side note.
