MEASURING A SHORTAGE

How often a station had zero bikes at commute time

Morning shortage

On weekdays with no rain from 06:00 to 10:00, the share of the 07:00, 08:00 and 09:00 inventory records that show zero bikes.

Rain comes from the nearest KMA station (median 2 km away). Days with missing rain records are dropped.

Frequent shortage

30% or more

9.8% of the 2,713 stations

The result is the same with a 20% or 40% cut-off.

Why drop wet mornings: rain cuts morning rentals by 51 to 89%, so demand on those days is different.

STA3115 Exploratory Data Analysis, Group 1

Here is how we measure a shortage. Inventory is recorded once an hour, so we take the 7, 8 and 9 a.m. records and ask how often the station had zero bikes. Rain cuts morning rentals by more than half, which changes demand itself, so we drop days when it rained during the commute. If a station is empty 30% of the time or more, we call it a frequent shortage; that is 9.8% of stations. Moving the cut-off to 20% or 40% gives the same result.
