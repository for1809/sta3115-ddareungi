STA3115 Exploratory Data Analysis, Group 1

Check four, housing. From Seoul’s living population data we built a residential index for each neighbourhood: night-time population divided by day-time population. Above one means people leave during the day. This coplot splits stations into thirds by that index, and in all three, business side or residential side, shortage rises with elevation. Adding the index to the regression leaves the elevation effect unchanged, 3.03 to 3.01, and the index itself has no effect. The population API only serves recent data, so we used June and July 2026.
