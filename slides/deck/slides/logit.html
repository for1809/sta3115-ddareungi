This is a logistic regression with subway distance, university distance and the number of docks. Every 10 metres of elevation multiplies the odds of being a frequent-shortage station by 2.1. Distance to the subway and to universities shows no relationship, and stations with more docks are empty slightly less often. The result holds month by month, between 1.75 and 2.18 in all twelve months, with a 20% or 40% cut-off, and with bare-earth elevation.
