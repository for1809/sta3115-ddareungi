STA3115 Exploratory Data Analysis, Group 1

Could it be the subway? Stations near a subway exit lose bikes fast at commute time. So we split stations into four groups by distance to the nearest subway station and drew a coplot. In all four panels, shortage rises with elevation. The shape differs a little: the curve is steeper in the panels closer to the subway, and in the logistic regression that interaction has p below 0.001.
