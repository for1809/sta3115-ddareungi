LIMITS

What this analysis cannot tell us

Not causal

Elevation held up after adding the residential index, but unobserved neighbourhood differences may remain.

Lost demand is invisible

Ride counts depend on supply. A shortage is only a trace of unmet demand.

Inventory is hourly

A station that empties and refills within the hour is missed.

No optimal fleet size

We show where bikes are short, not how many are needed.

STA3115 Exploratory Data Analysis, Group 1

There are limits. This is observational data, so we do not claim causation. Demand that met an empty station is not observed directly; a shortage is only its trace. Inventory is recorded once an hour, so a station that empties and refills in between is missed. And we did not estimate how many bikes the system should have.
