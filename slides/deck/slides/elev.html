STA3115 Exploratory Data Analysis, Group 1

The x axis is station elevation, the y axis is morning shortage, and the red line is a LOESS fit. It is flat below 20 metres and climbs steeply from about 25. By band, stations below 20 metres are empty 4.8% of the time, 20 to 40 metres 9.5%, and 40 metres and above 25.1%. A hill-top station is empty roughly one commute hour in four.
