Now back to our question. We treat a station as a merge candidate if it is in the bottom 20% of use and has another station within 300 metres. There are 427. But 128 of them, 30%, are often empty in the morning, and 95 of those are at 40 metres or higher. Among hill-top candidates, half already run empty. So for many of these stations, low use may mean no bikes, not no demand. Before we trust this, we need to check that it really is elevation.
