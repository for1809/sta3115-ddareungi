THREE OBJECTIONS

Is it really the hills?

1

Isn’t it the slope, not the height?

2

Isn’t it rainy days shaking demand?

3

Isn’t it that hill-tops are residential, so bikes leave every morning?

At this point you might raise three objections. One: maybe it is the steep slope, not the height. Two: maybe rainy days are shaking up demand. Three: maybe hill-tops are simply residential, so everyone rides down in the morning. We checked each one.
