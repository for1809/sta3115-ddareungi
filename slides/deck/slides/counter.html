Before we believe it, four checks. One: maybe it is the subway, since stations near an exit drain fast. Two: maybe it is the steep slope, not the height. Three: maybe rainy days are shaking up demand. Four: maybe hill-tops are simply residential, so everyone rides down in the morning. We take them one by one, and then put everything into one model.
